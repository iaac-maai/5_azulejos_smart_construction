# Ullada — Mission

## Problem

At the first meeting, clients bring AI or Pinterest images of a new kitchen or bathroom and ask what it will cost. The architect has no drawings and no quick way to price it, let alone an energy upgrade next to it. So the client picks cosmetic work only, and the cheapest moment to upgrade an inefficient flat (walls open, builders on site) passes for decades.

- About 9 in 10 Spanish homes are rated E–G.
- The EPBD requires a 16% cut in average home energy use by 2030, 55% of it from the worst buildings.
- Spain needs ~300,000 energy renovations a year to hit 1.2M by 2030 and does ~30,000.

## Solution

Photo + address in → two priced options out, side by side, in under 10 minutes:

| | Cosmetic | Efficient |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Kitchen + bathroom | Cosmetic + windows |
| Shows | Cost range | Cost range, label before/after, kWh and CO₂ saved, permit |

The AI reads the image and asks only the questions that change the answer. Catastro fills in the building. Unit-price databases set the costs. A deterministic engine does the maths. The architect checks every result.

## Users

- **Primary:** architects in small Barcelona studios, first client meeting.
- **Secondary:** their clients (owner-occupiers), who see the comparison.

## Scope (v1)

- Flats in multi-unit residential buildings, Barcelona, built 1950–1980.
- Rooms: kitchen, bathroom. Measure: windows.
- Out of scope: new builds, whole-building works, heat pumps and insulation (the later "deep" option), legally binding quotes.

## Objectives and key results (final review, 17 Dec)

**O1. Make energy upgrades part of the first conversation.**
- Photo + address → two options in < 10 minutes.
- Every energy figure traced to a cited source.
- Share of test sessions where the efficient option is chosen vs. today's baseline (from interviews).

**O2. Give ranges architects can trust.**
- ≈80% of real costs fall inside the range on ≥ 15 held-back cases.
- Median error beats both the €/m² rule and architects' own estimates.
- Image reading agrees with architects ≥ 60% (below that, rethink the image step).

**O3. Prove it gets used.**
- ≥ 4 architect interviews and ≥ 5 contractor quotes by 3 Dec.
- 1 architect runs a realistic session; SUS ≥ 70.
- Live demo on 17 Dec with every number linked to its source.

## Principles

1. Honest over precise: a wide true range beats a narrow wrong one.
2. The architect decides; Ullada prepares.
3. Every number has a source.
4. Small and working beats big and planned.
